# Software incumbents facing AI substitution

## Separate adoption from paid economics
Segment professional/enterprise workflows from casual individual creation. Precision editing, asset compatibility, collaboration, governance and repeatable delivery may preserve switching costs even as lightweight tasks commoditize. Neither enterprise retention nor consumer churn should be extrapolated to all customers.

Use: paid customers × realized net price + usage revenue. Track seats, paid conversion, renewals and inference costs separately from free MAU, generations and credits consumed. A free-access partnership's stated retail value is not booked revenue, cash receipts or an order backlog.

## Evidence reconciliation
- Reconcile reported versus constant-currency growth and organic versus acquisition-inclusive ARR before declaring a contradiction. Prefer official call transcripts and filing notes over abbreviated broker wording.
- Two brokers may both raise EPS but disagree on target prices because of terminal multiples, buyback/share-count assumptions and confidence in pricing power. Do not average away the dispute.
- Refresh leadership events and market prices before reusing older research upside. Announcing a successor resolves candidate uncertainty, not execution risk.
- Missing cohort conversion, realized pricing or renewal evidence remains a gap; do not invent measurable-looking retention thresholds from undisclosed data.

## Cash-flow bridge for valuation handoff
Compute TTM = latest fiscal year + current year-to-date − prior comparable year-to-date, separately for OCF, capex and SBC. Compute standalone quarters by cumulative differences; inspect half-year/TTM trends before blaming a quarterly tax or working-capital movement on competition.

Present OCF−capex and a conservative OCF−capex−SBC owner-cash-flow proxy. Explain tax and dilution simplifications. Vendor levered FCF is not necessarily OCF−capex. Reconcile repurchase cash with separately reported equity-award withholding taxes rather than treating all equity-related cash outflows as stock buybacks.

Avoid adding buyback yield to EPS growth already reflecting shrinking shares. High ROE can reflect repurchases reducing equity; it is not proof of high incremental returns. Recurring acquisitions required to sustain the business may warrant additional owner-cash deductions.

Hand off to valuation skills: equity cash-flow discounting must not mechanically repeat net-debt subtraction; GAAP/adjusted and forecast-period differences can explain trailing/forward PE divergence without a data error. Distinguish a required-return entry price from a safety-margin entry price, and do not lower the risk class merely to justify a desired price.

## Verified source-retrieval pattern
For Adobe filings, official financial-documents HTML exposes links to PDFs under `https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/`. Discover links from that page rather than guessing release slugs. An HTTP-success newsroom homepage is not the requested earnings article: verify title, reporting period and body. If extraction fails, direct HTTP/1.1 retrieval and PDF text extraction can be used; verify actual PDF content and cite physical pages. Keep filenames discovered from the current index, not frozen quarterly URLs.
