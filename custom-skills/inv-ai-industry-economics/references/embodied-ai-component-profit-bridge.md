# Embodied AI: industry demand to attributable supplier profit

Use for robotics sensors, actuators, reducers, machine vision, and other AI hardware components. This is an evidence and unit-economics framework, not a stock recommendation.

## 1. Evidence ledger before valuation

For each claim record source/date, PDF page sequence, printed page if different, underlying source, geography, period, unit, and status:
- Historical shipments: distinguish internal production, external deliveries, acceptance, and paid repeat purchases.
- Real operations: record fleet size, intervention rate, utilization, uptime, and customer payback only when disclosed.
- Orders/backlog: not equivalent to accepted deliveries or cash collection.
- Forecasts, capacity, company targets, demos, sample qualification, and MOUs: keep outside the historical-delivery column.

Broker authorship and underlying evidence are separate independence dimensions. A translated report is one source; two brokers quoting a prospectus are independent interpretations, not two independent measurements. Follow supply-map footnotes: “confirmed suppliers and sample suppliers” does not establish which individual company has volume orders.

## 2. Profit bridge

Build by OEM and form factor, avoiding double counting:

`accepted robot units × applicable design fraction × components per robot × outsourced fraction × supplier share × realized component ASP = supplier revenue`

Then subtract product COGS, incremental R&D, calibration, selling/service/warranty costs, depreciation, and tax. Apply minority interests or equity-method accounting as appropriate to obtain attributable earnings. Deduct incremental working capital and capex to assess cash returns; profit is not FCF.

Do not multiply a consolidated net margin by an ownership percentage mechanically when product economics and legal entities differ. Subsidiary revenue and equity-method associate revenue require different treatment. Explicitly reconcile consolidated net income to parent-attributable net income.

## 3. Price and BOM discipline

- Retail/list price, realized OEM ASP, manufacturing BOM cost, purchased finished components, and raw-material procurement are different measures.
- A vertically integrated OEM's external finished-component ratio is not its total external raw-material ratio. Preserve exclusions such as optional hands or premium lidar.
- Full-size bipeds, small bipeds, wheeled robots, and collection platforms have different configurations. Do not impose a universal sensor count; wheeled robots may still have arms.
- Force/tactile category value is not synonymous with six-axis force sensors or one company's addressable revenue.
- Model deflation and company cost-down targets are not observations of historical same-specification prices.
- If a stacked chart's colors and labels are lost in text extraction, verify the image or leave the percentages unknown.

Stress-test volume, ASP, outsourcing, share, margin, and dilution separately. Label all assumed ranges as hypotheses and name the evidence needed to replace them. Unknown component counts and prices should remain unknown rather than receive invented authoritative ranges.

## 4. Automated valuation audit

Check the raw data contract against rendered reports:
- `pe_static` must not become TTM/trailing PE merely because the report labels its generic anchor that way.
- Price-history percentile is not historical PE/PB percentile.
- Consolidated net margin is not parent-attributable net margin.
- Half-year ROE and annual ROE are not directly comparable.
- Fair-value changes, associate investment income, and minority interests can dominate reported growth.
- A successful exit code does not imply complete valuation evidence. Preserve partial status, gaps, and null action references.

For an explicitly requested return hurdle, with no dividends/dilution and comparable earnings definitions:

`required earnings CAGR = (1 + target annual return) × (entry PE / exit PE)^(1 / years) - 1`

Run the calculation with tools and mark it as sensitivity analysis, not a forecast. Do not invent an investor return hurdle or portfolio substitution instruction.

## 5. Document verification and bounded delivery

A successful download, PDF extension, or parser-created single page does not establish a valid filing. Confirm content type/decoded bytes, plausible page count, and substantive text or rendered pages. Filing metadata can establish title, publication date, and URL, but cannot establish whether a revenue segment is disclosed. If正文 is unverified, report **not verified**, not **not disclosed**.

For a timeboxed review, select the strongest candidate, run actual data/valuation, retain raw outputs, prioritize decisive source pages, and reserve time to finish and check the requested artifact. Report extraction coverage versus close-reading/visual coverage honestly. If current comparable prices or segment data are missing, give a conditional research comparison rather than a fabricated numerical ranking.

## Session-derived lessons (2026-09; dated, not evergreen investment facts)

A robotics review independently found evidence of high OEM gross margins alongside forecast profit contraction caused by greater model/operating spending, and a high degree of in-house component production. These are useful counterexamples to shipment-to-profit extrapolation, not universal ratios to reuse. The same review caught a static-PE/trailing-label mismatch and consolidated-versus-parent margin mismatch in automated outputs. Revalidate all actual numbers from current source documents; preserve the methodology, not stale stock prices or issuer forecasts.
